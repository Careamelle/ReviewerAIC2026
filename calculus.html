Differential Calculus (Math 10-01 to 10-11)

1. Evaluate lim (x → ∞) (3x² + 2x + 1) / (x² + x + 1)
Answer: B. 3
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Divide the numerator and denominator by the highest power of x, which is x².
  • This yields: (3 + 2/x + 1/x²) / (1 + 1/x + 1/x²).
  • As x approaches infinity, any term with x in the denominator approaches 0.
  • The limit simplifies to the ratio of the leading coefficients: 3 / 1 = 3.
2. Find the equation of the tangent line to y = x³ - 2x² + 2 at x = 1
Answer: A. y = -x + 2
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Find the y-coordinate at x = 1: y = (1)³ - 2(1)² + 2 = 1. The point is (1, 1).
  • Find the derivative to get the slope (m): y' = 3x² - 4x.
  • Substitute x = 1 into the derivative: m = 3(1)² - 4(1) = -1.
  • Use the point-slope form y - y↑ = m(x - x↑): y - 1 = -1(x - 1).
  • Simplify: y = -x + 1 + 1 → y = -x + 2.
3. Water is flowing into a conical vessel 15 cm deep and having a radius of 3.75 cm across the top. If the rate at which water is rising is 2 cm/sec. How fast is the water flowing into the conical vessel when the depth of water is 4 cm?
Answer: B. 6.28 m³/min (Note: units in choices likely meant cm³/sec)
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Use similar triangles to find the radius-to-height ratio: r / h = 3.75 / 15 = 1 / 4, so r = h / 4.
  • Substitute r into the volume formula of a cone: V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)π(h/4)²h = (π/48)h³.
  • Differentiate with respect to time (t): dV/dt = (π/16)h² (dh/dt).
  • Substitute the given values (h = 4, dh/dt = 2): dV/dt = (π/16)(4)²(2) = 2π ≈ 6.28.
4. Find the minimum amount of tin sheet that can be made into a closed cylinder having a volume of 108 cu. inches in square inches.
Answer: A. 125.50
Step-by-step Solution:
  • For a closed cylinder to have minimum surface area for a given volume, the height must equal the diameter: h = 2r.
  • Substitute into volume: V = πr²h = πr²(2r) = 2πr³ = 108.
  • Solve for r: r³ = 54/π → r ≈ 2.58 inches.
  • Calculate total surface area: A = 2πr² + 2πrh = 6πr².
  • A = 6π(2.58)² ≈ 125.50 sq. in.
5. Given a cone of diameter x and altitude of h. What percent is the volume of the largest cylinder which can be inscribed in the cone to the volume of the cone?
Answer: A. 44%
Step-by-step Solution:
  • By optimization principles, the radius of the largest inscribed cylinder is 2/3 the radius of the cone, and its height is 1/3 the height of the cone.
  • Volume of Cone: Vc = (1/3)πR²H
  • Volume of max Cylinder: Vy = π(2R/3)²(H/3) = π(4R²/9)(H/3) = (4/27)πR²H
  • Ratio: Vy / Vc = (4/27) / (1/3) = 4/9.
  • Convert to percent: 4/9 ≈ 0.4444 or 44.4%.
6. A balloon is released from the ground 100 meters from an observer. The balloon rises directly upward at the rate of 4 meters per second. How fast is the balloon receding from the observer 10 seconds later?
Answer: D. 1.49 m/sec
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Form a right triangle where horizontal distance x = 100 m (constant), and vertical height y = 4t.
  • After 10 seconds, y = 4(10) = 40 m.
  • Distance between observer and balloon (s): s = √(100² + 40²) = √(11600) ≈ 107.7 m.
  • Using Pythagorean theorem derivative: s² = x² + y² → 2s(ds/dt) = 2x(dx/dt) + 2y(dy/dt).
  • Since dx/dt = 0 (observer is stationary): ds/dt = (y * dy/dt) / s = (40 * 4) / 107.7 ≈ 1.49 m/s.
7. A box is to be constructed from a piece of zinc 20 sq.in by cutting equal squares from each corner and turning up the zinc to form the side. What is the volume of the largest box that can be so constructed?
Answer: B. 592.59 cu in.
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Assuming the material is a 20x20 inch square based on the choices. Let x be the side of the cut squares.
  • Volume equation: V = x(20 - 2x)².
  • Take the derivative and set to zero to optimize: V' = 12x² - 160x + 400 = 0.
  • Solving the quadratic gives x = 10/3 (x=10 is rejected as it leaves no base).
  • Substitute x back into Volume: V = (10/3)(20 - 20/3)² = (10/3)(40/3)² = 16000/27 ≈ 592.59 cu. in.
8. A statue 3 m high is standing on a base of 4 m high. If an observer's eye is 1.5 m above the ground, how far should he stand from the base in order that the angle subtended by the statue is a maximum.
Answer: C. 3.71 m
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Determine the vertical heights from the observer's eye level.
  • Top of the statue from eye: y↑ = 4 + 3 - 1.5 = 5.5 m.
  • Bottom of the statue from eye: y↓ = 4 - 1.5 = 2.5 m.
  • The optimum distance x for maximum viewing angle is the geometric mean of these two heights: x = √(y↑ * y↓).
  • x = √(5.5 * 2.5) = √(13.75) ≈ 3.71 m.
9. Water is running into a hemispherical bowl having a radius of 10 cm at a constant rate of 3 cm³/min. When the water is x cm. deep, the water level is rising at the rate of 0.0149 cm/min. What is the value of x?
Answer: C. 4
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Volume of a spherical cap: V = π(Rx² - x³/3) where R = 10.
  • Differentiate with respect to time: dV/dt = π(2Rx - x²) * (dx/dt).
  • Substitute knowns (dV/dt = 3, R = 10, dx/dt = 0.0149): 3 = π(20x - x²)(0.0149).
  • Simplify: 20x - x² = 3 / (π * 0.0149) ≈ 64.
  • Rearrange to quadratic: x² - 20x + 64 = 0. Factoring gives (x - 4)(x - 16) = 0.
  • Since x must be less than R (10), x = 4 cm.
10. Evaluate: lim (x → 1) (x² - 1) / (x² + 3x - 4)
Answer: B. 2/5
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Direct substitution yields 0/0. Factor both the numerator and denominator.
  • Numerator: (x - 1)(x + 1)
  • Denominator: (x - 1)(x + 4)
  • Cancel the common (x - 1) term to get: (x + 1) / (x + 4).
  • Substitute x = 1: (1 + 1) / (1 + 4) = 2/5.
11. Evaluate: lim (x → 0) (1 - cos x) / x²
Answer: B. 1/2
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Direct substitution yields 0/0. Apply L'Hôpital's Rule (differentiate top and bottom).
  • First derivative: (sin x) / (2x). At x = 0, this is still 0/0.
  • Apply L'Hôpital's Rule again: (cos x) / 2.
  • Substitute x = 0: (cos 0) / 2 = 1/2.
12. What is the derivative with respect to x of (x + 1)³ - x³?
Answer: D. 6x + 3
Step-by-step Solution:
  • First, expand the term (x + 1)³ = x³ + 3x² + 3x + 1.
  • Subtract x³: (x³ + 3x² + 3x + 1) - x³ = 3x² + 3x + 1.
  • Take the derivative of the simplified polynomial: d/dx (3x² + 3x + 1) = 6x + 3.
13. Find y' if y = arcsin(cos x)
Answer: A. -1
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Use the trigonometric identity: cos x = sin(π/2 - x).
  • Substitute into the equation: y = arcsin(sin(π/2 - x)).
  • This simplifies directly to: y = π/2 - x.
  • Take the derivative with respect to x: y' = -1.
14. Differentiate the equation: y = x² / (x + 1)
Answer: A. (x² + 2x) / (x + 1)²
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Apply the Quotient Rule: d/dx [u/v] = (vu' - uv') / v².
  • Here, u = x² (so u' = 2x) and v = x + 1 (so v' = 1).
  • Plug into the rule: y' = [(x + 1)(2x) - (x²)(1)] / (x + 1)².
  • Simplify numerator: 2x² + 2x - x² = x² + 2x.
  • Final answer: (x² + 2x) / (x + 1)².
15. Give the slope of the curve at the point (1,1): y = x³/4 - 2x + 1
Answer: D. -1 1/4 (or -1.25)
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Find the first derivative to get the slope equation: y' = 3x²/4 - 2.

  • 3/4 - 8/4 = -5/4 = -1.25 or -1 1/4.
16. Find the equation of the normal to x² + y² = 5 at the point (2,1).
Answer: B. x = 2y
Step-by-step Solution:
  • This equation represents a circle centered at the origin (0,0).
  • A geometrical property of circles is that the normal line at any point always passes through the center.
  • Find the line passing through (0,0) and (2,1).
  • Slope m = (1 - 0) / (2 - 0) = 1/2.
  • Equation: y = (1/2)x → 2y = x.
17. At point of inflection,
Answer: B. y'' = 0
Step-by-step Solution:
  • A point of inflection occurs where the concavity of a function changes.
  • Concavity is determined by the second derivative. Therefore, at the exact point it changes, y'' = 0 (or is undefined).
18. The ___ derivative of the function is the rate of change of the slope of the graph.
Answer: B. second
Step-by-step Solution:
  • The first derivative represents the slope of the graph.
  • The rate of change of that slope is the derivative of the first derivative, which is defined as the second derivative.
19. Two posts, one 8 m and the other 12 m high are 15 m apart. If the posts are supported by a cable running from the top of the first posts to a stake on the ground and then to the top of the second post, find the distance from the lower post to the stake to use minimum amount of cable.
Answer: A. 6 m from the lower post
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Use the reflection principle: conceptually reflect the 8m post below the ground to create a straight-line path for the cable.
  • By similar triangles, the ratio of height to base must be equal: 8 / x = 12 / (15 - x).
  • Cross-multiply: 8(15 - x) = 12x → 120 - 8x = 12x.
  • 120 = 20x → x = 6 m.
20. The distance a body travels is a function of time t and is defined by: x(t) = 18t + 9t². What is its velocity at t = 3?
Answer: B. 72
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Velocity is the first derivative of the position function: v(t) = x'(t).
  • Differentiate: v(t) = 18 + 18t.
  • Substitute t = 3: v(3) = 18 + 18(3) = 18 + 54 = 72.
21. When the area in square units of an expanding circle is increasing twice as fast as its radius in linear unit, the radius is
Answer: B. 1/π
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Area of a circle: A = πr².
  • Differentiate with respect to time: dA/dt = 2πr (dr/dt).
  • We are given that dA/dt = 2(dr/dt).
  • Substitute this into the derivative equation: 2(dr/dt) = 2πr (dr/dt).
  • Divide both sides by 2(dr/dt) to yield: 1 = πr → r = 1/π.
22. The depth of water in a cylindrical tank 4 m in diameter is increasing at the rate of 0.7 m/min. Find the rate at which the water flowing into the tank.
Answer: D. 8.7964 cu. m/min
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Diameter = 4 m, so radius r = 2 m.
  • Volume of cylinder: V = πr²h.
  • Differentiate with respect to time: dV/dt = πr²(dh/dt) (radius is constant).
  • Substitute known values: dV/dt = π(2)²(0.7) = 4π(0.7) = 2.8π.
  • 2.8 * 3.14159 ≈ 8.7964 m³/min.
23. Evaluate d/dx (ln e2x).
Answer: D. 2
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Before differentiating, simplify the expression using logarithm rules.
  • The natural log and exponential function are inverses: ln(e^u) = u.
  • Therefore, ln(e2x) = 2x.
  • Now take the derivative: d/dx (2x) = 2.
24. An open rectangular box with square base is to be made from 48 sq. m of material. What is the largest possible volume of the box in cubic meters?
Answer: C. 32
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Let x be the side of the square base, and h be the height. Surface area for an open box: A = x² + 4xh = 48.
  • Solve for h: h = (48 - x²) / 4x.
  • Substitute h into the Volume equation: V = x²h = x²[(48 - x²) / 4x] = 12x - x³/4.
  • Take derivative and set to zero: V' = 12 - 3x²/4 = 0.
  • Solve for x: 3x²/4 = 12 → x² = 16 → x = 4.
  • Calculate Max Volume: V = 12(4) - (4)³/4 = 48 - 16 = 32 m³.
25. The volume of a cylindrical tin can with a top and a bottom is to be 16 cubic inches. If a minimum amount of tin is to be used to construct the can, what must be the height, in inches, of the can?
Answer: A. 4
Step-by-step Solution:
  • Assume the volume given is meant to be 16π cubic inches (a standard convention in such textbook problems yielding an integer answer).
  • For a closed cylinder to use minimum material, the height must equal the diameter: h = 2r.
  • Substitute into volume: V = πr²h = πr²(2r) = 2πr³ = 16π.
  • Solve for r: 2r³ = 16 → r³ = 8 → r = 2 inches.
  • Calculate height: h = 2(2) = 4 inches.
